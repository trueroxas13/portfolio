# The Player Experience
The player will undergo a journey through the lands, performing the following actions:
   * __Fighting__ Monsters
   * __Opening__ Chests
     + *Potions, Experience, Loot...*

Doing so empowers the player character, granting them access to greater heights of power. Below you may find the player stat scaling:
